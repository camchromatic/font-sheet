Tips and tricks

Start

  • Drop a font file on the box, or click it to choose one. It takes .ttf, .otf, .woff and .woff2.
  • Drop another font at any time to swap it. Your settings stay the same.
  • The card appears on the right (below, on narrow screens).

Sections

  • Tick or untick each section to put it on the card or take it off.
  • Options ▸ next to a section opens its extras: Light on dark, spacing, and more.
  • Light on dark turns that section dark with light text.
  • Spacing: type Letters (−10 to 50%) and Lines (80 to 200%). Reset all spacing puts them all back.

Text

  • Pick a pangram (a sentence with every letter), or press Random.
  • Type in Your own text to add it to the card. It's skipped while the box is empty.

Missing letters

  • Letters the font doesn't have are drawn pale grey, and a note lists them.
  • This can't be checked for .woff2 files or old symbol fonts.
  • The Glyph grid shows every character the font has.

Save and share

  • Card size: Fit to content, or a social media size (Square, Portrait, Story, Link preview). Big cards shrink to fit; untick sections to make the text bigger.
  • Save as PNG makes a sharp picture. Save as PDF makes a 1-page PDF the same size.
  • Check the font's licence before you share cards of it publicly.

Good to know

  • Everything runs in your browser. Your font is never uploaded, there's no sign-in, and no AI is used.
  • Your sections, options, text and Theme are remembered in this browser.
